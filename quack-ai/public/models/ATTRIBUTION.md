# 3D model attribution

`duck.glb` is the classic Khronos glTF Sample Models "Duck", originally
published by Sony Computer Entertainment Inc. (2006) and redistributed as part
of the [KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models)
repository. Used here as a placeholder product mesh for a satirical demo;
materials are overridden at runtime.
